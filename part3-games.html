Part 3 — 100-game milestone

100 distinct gameplay designs in the catalogue: 31 Part 1 standalone games + the preserved original Vector-Physics Pac-Man and Candy Cascade + 67 new Part 3 games. Part 2’s repeated-mechanic pages are retained in the archive but not counted again.

This is the cumulative catalogue toward 500. Static syntax/archive checks do not equal full browser playtesting; this build still needs interactive testing and polish.

Preserved original games

  1. Vector-Physics Pac-Man — Original maze-chase game preserved.
  2. Candy Cascade — Original match-three game preserved.

67 new Part 3 games

  1. Hangman Hologram — Guess the hidden word one letter at a time before the energy meter empties.
  2. Word Search Nebula — Find the target words hidden horizontally, vertically, or diagonally in the letter grid.
  3. Anagram Reactor — Rearrange the scrambled letters into the correct word.
  4. Word Ladder Lab — Change one letter at a time to transform COLD into WARM using valid four-letter words.
  5. Cipher Vault — Crack the hidden four-colour code. Feedback shows correct colour in the right place and correct colour elsewhere.
  6. Lights Out Matrix — Tap a tile to toggle it and its neighbours. Turn every light off.
  7. Hanoi Neon Towers — Move all rings from the first peg to the third. Never place a larger ring on a smaller one.
  8. Peg Solitaire Star — Jump pegs over neighbouring pegs into empty holes to remove the jumped peg. Finish with one peg.
  9. Nimfire — Take 1–3 crystals from the pile. Whoever takes the last crystal wins.
  10. Matchstick Equation — Move exactly one matchstick to make the displayed equation correct.
  11. River Crossing Crew — Transport the fox, chicken, and grain across the river. The boat carries you plus one item; fox and chicken or chicken and grain cannot be left alone.
  12. Water Sort Lab — Pour the top colour between tubes. Sort each tube into a single colour.
  13. Pipeworks Circuit — Rotate pipe tiles to connect the source to the destination.
  14. Laser Labyrinth — Rotate mirrors to guide the laser from emitter to receiver.
  15. Tangram Orbit — Build a target silhouette by choosing and rotating geometric pieces.
  16. Chromatic Alchemist — Mix primary colours to create the requested colour.
  17. Orbit Pattern Logic — Choose the missing symbol in the evolving pattern.
  18. Balance Bot — Balance the scale by selecting weights that match the target mass.
  19. Higher or Lower Galaxy — Predict whether the next card will be higher or lower than the current card.
  20. Blackjack Neon Table — Get as close to 21 as possible without busting. Dealer stands on 17.
  21. Dice Forge Five — Roll five dice up to three times. Lock dice you want to keep; aim for five of a kind.
  22. War of the Decks — Flip one card each round. Highest card wins the round; collect the most wins in ten rounds.
  23. Spelling Bee Beacon — Listen to the clue and spell the word correctly.
  24. Rhyme Time Arena — Choose the word that rhymes with the prompt.
  25. Cosmic Quiz Show — Answer quick space and science trivia to build your score.
  26. Mole Patrol — Tap the glowing mole before it disappears. Earn points in 20 seconds.
  27. Bubble Pop Geometry — Aim a bubble into a matching cluster to pop groups of three or more.
  28. Pinball Prism — Launch a pinball and tap the flippers to keep it bouncing through bumpers.
  29. Mini Golf Comet — Put the ball into the hole with a single shot. Choose power and direction.
  30. Slalom Sprint — Steer left and right to pass through gates without hitting flags.
  31. Traffic Threader — Switch lanes to avoid traffic for as long as possible.
  32. Cave Copter — Hold to rise and release to fall. Fly through the cave without touching its walls.
  33. Submarine Sonar — Use sonar pings to locate the hidden submarine. Guess its grid coordinate in as few pings as possible.
  34. Deep Sea Catch — Tap when the bobber plunges to catch a fish; early or late taps miss.
  35. Target Toss — Throw a ball with a chosen angle and power to land inside the target ring.
  36. Artillery Angle — Set elevation and charge to hit the distant bunker. Wind changes each shot.
  37. Shadow Sneak — Reach the exit without stepping into a guard’s line of sight.
  38. Micro Tower Defense — Place turrets along the path and stop waves of creeps reaching the base.
  39. Pocket Farm Manager — Plant seeds, harvest crops, and earn coins to expand your tiny farm.
  40. Crystal Miner — Mine blocks to uncover crystals. Pick the block with the strongest shimmer clue.
  41. Factory Flow — Rotate conveyor arrows so parts travel from the source to the assembly station.
  42. Train Dispatcher — Switch track junctions to guide each train to the matching-colour station.
  43. Runway Control — Assign incoming planes to clear runways without sending two planes to the same strip.
  44. Ecosystem Balance — Adjust plant, herbivore, and predator populations to keep the ecosystem balanced for five seasons.
  45. Ant Colony Architect — Place food sources and guide ants along the shortest route back to the nest.
  46. Quantum Clicker — Generate energy, buy multipliers, and reach one million energy.
  47. Arcade Shopkeeper — Buy stock, set a price, and sell items to satisfy customers while earning profit.
  48. Dungeon Tactics — Defeat a guard by choosing attack, defend, or potion at the right moment.
  49. Grid Command Tactics — Move your unit to capture the control point while avoiding enemy zones.
  50. Rogue Room Run — Explore a tiny dungeon, collect a key, and reach the exit while avoiding traps.
  51. Lockpick Resonance — Tune three tumblers to the correct numbers using warm/cold feedback.
  52. Safecracker Dial — Stop the rotating dial in the three target zones to open the safe.
  53. Conductor’s Baton — Conduct a four-beat pattern by tapping the correct beat buttons in order.
  54. Drum Fill Duel — Match the drummer’s requested rhythm by choosing kick, snare, and cymbal hits.
  55. Type Racer Sprint — Type the sentence accurately before the timer runs out.
  56. Reflex Reactor — Tap only when the signal turns green. False starts cost a life.
  57. Pixel Aim Trainer — Hit the moving targets before they vanish.
  58. Shape Rotation Lab — Rotate the shape to match the target orientation.
  59. Gravity Flip Runner — Switch gravity to avoid spikes on both floor and ceiling.
  60. Bridge Builder Mini — Place supports under the bridge to carry a moving truck across the gap.
  61. Water Jug Riddle — Measure exactly four units using 3-unit and 5-unit jugs. Fill, empty, and pour between them.
  62. Intersection Controller — Change traffic lights to prevent collisions while letting cars cross.
  63. Paper Plane Navigator — Adjust pitch to glide through rings while managing altitude and speed.
  64. Constellation Connect — Connect the stars in the requested order without lifting your finger.
  65. Orbit Stabilizer — Adjust orbital speed to keep a satellite inside a stable band for ten ticks.
  66. Cargo Bay Tetris — Load cargo crates into a ship with limited capacity without exceeding the weight limit.
  67. Timeblock Tactician — Fit tasks into a six-slot schedule without overlaps; each task has a time window.
