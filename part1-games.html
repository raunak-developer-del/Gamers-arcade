Gamers’ Arcade — Part 1

31 planned playable games: 10 original mechanics, classic arcade titles, board games, and more original challenges.

Preserved legacy games: Original Pac-Man · Original Candy Cascade
  1. Neon Needle — Stop the moving needle inside the green target zone. Timing—not movement—is the core mechanic.
  2. Wordstorm — Type each falling word before it reaches the bottom. Keyboard accuracy is the challenge.
  3. Echo Sequence — Memorize and repeat a growing sequence of four tones/colors. Memory sequence game, not a matching-pairs game.
  4. Pixel Nonogram — Use row and column clues to reveal the hidden pixel pattern. Logic-grid deduction, not mine finding.
  5. Operator Lab — Choose the missing operator to make the equation true. A quick logic and arithmetic challenge.
  6. Lunar Landing — Control thrust and horizontal drift to land softly on the pad. Physics-based landing, not dodging.
  7. Mirror Maze — Find a path to the exit in a generated maze. Grid navigation and route planning.
  8. Beat Reactor — Tap when the pulse crosses the target ring. Rhythm and reaction timing.
  9. Skybound Platformer — Jump between platforms and collect three stars before falling. Precision platform movement.
  10. Stack Sync — Drop moving blocks and keep their overlapping edges aligned. Balance and precision stacking.
  11. Tetris Classic — Rotate and place falling tetrominoes to clear complete rows.
  12. Snake Classic — Steer a growing snake to eat food without hitting walls or its own body.
  13. Space Invaders — Move your ship and shoot descending alien waves.
  14. Breakout Classic — Bounce a ball with your paddle to clear a wall of bricks.
  15. Frogger Classic — Cross moving traffic and reach the safe bank.
  16. Pong Classic — Keep the ball in play and outscore the computer paddle.
  17. Chess — Play a local two-player chess game with legal piece movement patterns and captures.
  18. Checkers — Capture opposing pieces by jumping diagonally; reach the far edge to crown kings.
  19. Tic-Tac-Toe — Classic three-in-a-row game for two local players.
  20. Connect Four — Drop discs into columns and connect four horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  21. Reversi — Place discs to trap and flip opposing discs; most discs wins.
  22. 2048 — Slide numbered tiles, combine equal values, and reach 2048.
  23. Sudoku Mini — Fill each row and column with digits 1–4 without repeating.
  24. Solitaire Piles — Move cards between descending alternating-color piles; build each suit from Ace to King.
  25. Mahjong Pairs — Clear the board by matching identical exposed tile pairs.
  26. Asteroids Drift — Rotate, thrust, and shoot to break apart drifting asteroids.
  27. Flappy Pulse — Flap through moving gaps without hitting the pipes.
  28. Minesweeper Classic — Reveal safe squares and flag mines using adjacent mine counts.
  29. Gravity Flip — Flip gravity to move between floor and ceiling while avoiding obstacles.
  30. Drawbridge Engineer — Draw a bridge line to connect two platforms and guide the cart across.
  31. Pulse Archery — Time your shot and aim for the moving target to score bullseyes.

Chess and Checkers are local two-player builds. Chess uses basic movement/captures; advanced rules including castling, en passant and checkmate validation are not yet complete. Do not mistake this for tournament-grade chess.
