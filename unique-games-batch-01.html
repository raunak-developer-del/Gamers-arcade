Gamers’ Arcade — Distinct Game Batch 1

First 10 separately implemented games with distinct core mechanics.

  1. Neon Needle — Stop the moving needle inside the green target zone. Timing—not movement—is the core mechanic.
  2. Wordstorm — Type each falling word before it reaches the bottom. Keyboard accuracy is the challenge.
  3. Echo Sequence — Memorize and repeat a growing sequence of four tones/colors. Memory sequence game, not a matching-pairs game.
  4. Minefield Logic — Reveal safe cells and flag hidden mines. Deduction puzzle with adjacency counts.
  5. Operator Lab — Choose the missing operator to make the equation true. A quick logic and arithmetic challenge.
  6. Lunar Landing — Control thrust and horizontal drift to land softly on the pad. Physics-based landing, not dodging.
  7. Mirror Maze — Find a path to the exit in a generated maze. Grid navigation and route planning.
  8. Beat Reactor — Tap when the pulse crosses the target ring. Rhythm and reaction timing.
  9. Skybound Platformer — Jump between platforms and collect three stars before falling. Precision platform movement.
  10. Stack Sync — Drop moving blocks and keep their overlapping edges aligned. Balance and precision stacking.

These are newly implemented games. They do not yet represent all 500 requested games.
